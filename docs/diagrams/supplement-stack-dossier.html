00 — Executive summary

The stack sorts onto one axis: catecholaminergic AM, serotonergic / sedative PM

The optimization problem is not "what to add" — it is spacing collisions. Two windows are overloaded: an AM dopaminergic / adrenergic cluster stacked on the stimulant, and a PM serotonergic + sedative cluster stacked on the anxiolytic and sleep agents.

Top flags

9-Me-BC × Lisdexamfetamine — MAOI on a stimulantBeta-carbolines carry MAO-inhibiting activity. Any MAOI + amphetamine is the textbook hypertensive-crisis / serotonin-syndrome contraindication. Add L-tyrosine and you triple the catecholaminergic load. Single highest-risk edge in the stack.
Four serotonergic inputs converge in the PM5-HTP (Sakara) + L-tryptophan (Eight Sleep) + L-tryptophan (NOW, standalone) + buspirone (5-HT1A) — running all of them is serotonergic polypharmacy. The real PM redundancy is serotonin precursors, not melatonin.
Sedative / QT stacking at nightHydroxyzine (QT-prolonging) + guanfacine (hypotensive) + valerian sleep blend = additive sedation and blood-pressure drop. Pick one sleep product; avoid PRN hydroxyzine on the same night as a sedating blend.
One beneficial pairing already in placeGuanfacine × lisdexamfetamine is an evidence-based ADHD combo — the alpha-2A agonist blunts stimulant HR/BP and smooths the evening comedown. Keep it.

Stack constraints & interactive tools

Hard constraint: melatonin-freeBy design the stack stays melatonin-free — and it already does: Thorne Deep Sleep uses a melatonin analog (Maizinol / 6-MBOA), while Sakara and Eight Sleep lean on 5-HTP / tryptophan + tart cherry (trace only). No item here adds exogenous melatonin; keep it that way.
Coffee is modeledAM (~8 AM) and noon (~12 PM) coffee (~95 mg each) are now in the load + PK charts and the simulator. The noon cup (t½ ~5 h) is still ~25% active at bedtime — it opposes the sleep stack.

Magnesium: Glycinate (yours) vs Bisglycinate — same molecule"Glycinate" and "bisglycinate" both mean magnesium chelated to two glycines (bis = two) — chemically identical, identically gentle, identically calming (glycine is itself inhibitory). The real difference is form & label: Thorne's Glycinate is a capsule (fixed dose, travel-easy — but in cheaper brands watch for oxide-buffered "glycinate" that dilutes true chelate); the Bisglycinate is a monk-fruit powder, 200 mg elemental/scoop (dose-flexible, fully-reacted chelate). Verdict: keep the Glycinate caps you have for adherence; switch to powder only to dial the dose or avoid pills. Never run both — that's ~double the elemental Mg and a laxative threshold.
L-Carnitine: nootropic efficacy — wrong ester for the brainYour Beyond Raw is L-Carnitine L-Tartrate (LCLT) — validated for exercise recovery and reduced muscle damage, but it poorly crosses the blood-brain barrier, so direct cognitive effects are weak. The nootropic ester is Acetyl-L-Carnitine (ALCAR): it crosses the BBB, donates acetyl groups toward acetylcholine, and has modest human evidence for mental fatigue, processing speed, and mood (strongest in older adults / fatigue states, thin in healthy young adults). Net: LCLT gives systemic energy/recovery and may help cognition indirectly via better sleep + lower fatigue — but if cognition is the goal, ALCAR ~1.5–3 g is the targeted form. Don't expect LCLT to act as a nootropic.

08 — Action

Raise with prescriber & pharmacist

  • 9-Me-BC's MAO activity while on lisdexamfetamine — is co-dosing acceptable, or should 9-Me-BC be dropped?
  • Cumulative serotonergic load: standalone L-tryptophan + Sakara 5-HTP + Eight Sleep tryptophan + buspirone — which to keep.
  • L-tyrosine's value/risk while already on a stimulant.
  • QT review given PRN hydroxyzine alongside other agents; same-night sedation stacking with guanfacine + a sleep blend.
  • Dihexa: no human safety data + a c-Met/oncogenic theoretical concern — informed-risk discussion.
  • CoQ10 omega-3 confirmed (take AM); keep magnesium to one form (Glycinate caps) — don't double with bisglycinate.
  • Coffee load: AM + noon caffeine adds adrenergic load to the stimulant; the noon cup also erodes sleep — worth a cutoff.
