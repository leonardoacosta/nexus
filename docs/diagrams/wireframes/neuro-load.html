  • 1PIN 1 — AM dopaminergic peakMorning blue hump (~10 AM) = Vyvanse onset + first coffee + Semax stacking on the dopaminergic / adrenergic axis.
  • 2PIN 2 — PM serotonergic + sedative peakNight violet + teal humps (~10 PM) = serotonergic agents plus GABAergic / sedative wind-down piling into the same window.
  • 3how load is computedLoad is a qualitative sum of all active agents by neuro-system at each hour — not a pharmacokinetic curve, a relative pile-up heuristic.
  • 4live from dose eventsThe stack is recomputed from logged dose events × each substance's neuro-system tags — change a dose, the curve moves.
  • 5legend togglesToggling a system chip off drops that layer and recomputes the remaining stack — here Caffeine is off to isolate the prescription load.
  • 6hour scrubberThe scrubber pins a single hour (2:00 PM); the right panel answers "what's driving this hour" with per-agent contribution bars.
  • 7contribution barsEach bar is one agent's share of the selected hour's total load, mono % label — Lisdexamfetamine dominates the 2 PM slice.
  • 8caffeine's footprintCaffeine contributes twice — the AM ramp and the noon coffee — which is why disabling it visibly flattens both stimulant humps.
  • 9overloaded windowsWhen two same-system agents overlap (noon stimulant stack), the window earns a STACKED flag to surface piled-up load.
  • 1012-hour axisAxis is rendered in 12-hour clock (6A → 12A) to match how doses are actually logged through the day.
