  • 1SUMMARY BARTop-level count of distinct actives that appear in 2+ products (3 here). Severity badge escalates with the worst group below — mod when no acute risk, major when a syndrome-watch cluster exists.
  • 2CONSTRAINT CHIPThe melatonin-free hard constraint is enforced at this view. A green-on chip confirms the active stack satisfies it; the inline note proves none of the 3 sleep products silently reintroduces melatonin.
  • 3INGREDIENT DECOMPOSITIONEach duplicate group is keyed on a single active, not a product. Multi-ingredient products (Thorne Deep Sleep, Sakara blend) are decomposed into product_ingredient rows so a blend's L-Theanine lands in the same group as the standalone capsule.
  • 4SOURCE CHIPSEach chip = one product_ingredient row carrying its parent product + per-serving dose. This is the audit trail for how the combined total was reached.
  • 5DOSE ROLL-UP BARPer-group track sums the contributing doses into one combined figure (≈300+ mg theanine, ≈400 mg elemental Mg). Fill width is the combined load against a soft ceiling — visual cue for "this is the over-dose, not any single label."
  • 6SEROTONERGIC CLUSTERThe key flag of this screen: 4 converging serotonergic inputs (2× tryptophan + 5-HTP + an Rx 5-HT1A agonist) summed across OTC and Rx sources. This is mechanism stacking, not just same-ingredient doubling.
  • 7SYNDROME WATCHThe serotonin-syndrome watch is a major-severity flag attached to group 02 — raised because the cluster crosses the Rx boundary (Buspirone) where additive 5-HT load carries clinical risk.
  • 8SAME-CHELATE REDUNDANCYMagnesium group catches a subtler case: two products are the same chelate (glycinate ≡ bisglycinate are the same salt), so they are pure redundancy rather than complementary forms.
  • 9CONSOLIDATE ACTIONSEach group offers a single primary remediation — keep one source, reduce to one precursor, pick one form. The serotonergic action is promoted to a primary button because it carries the highest-severity flag.
  • 10MINOR DUPLICATESLow-stakes doubles (tart cherry ×2, ashwagandha ×2) are collapsed into one accent note rather than full cards — surfaced for completeness without competing with the actionable clusters.
