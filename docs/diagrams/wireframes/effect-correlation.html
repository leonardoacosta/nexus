  • 1PIN 1 — effect peak after doseThe red line peaks ~3.5 h after the morning Vyvanse pin — the visual overlay of effect_log ratings on top of dose_event markers is the whole point of the screen.
  • 2acute vs cumulative toggleThe .wf-seg lag toggle pivots on each substance's half_life: Acute scopes the correlation to within ~5 t½ of a dose; Cumulative looks at multi-day trend instead.
  • 3metric pickerThe "Metric: Focus rating" dropdown swaps which series overlays the dose timeline — one control, many metrics.
  • 4PIN 4 — dose pinsEach navy pin on the baseline is a logged dose_event (Vyvanse 7A, Coffee 8A, Coffee 12P) — the events the metric is correlated against.
  • 5correlation readoutThe accent panel surfaces a single Pearson r = 0.62 for the selected metric vs the active substance at the chosen lag.
  • 6lag scopes which doses countThe lag window decides which dose events fall inside the correlation: at the 3.5 h acute lag only the AM Vyvanse is in-window; the noon coffee is excluded from this r.
  • 7subjective + objective sourcesFocus / Anxiety / Sleep quality are subjective effect_log entries; Resting HR / HRV are objective pulls from Eight Sleep / a wearable — both plot identically.
  • 8insight generationThe one-liner ("Focus peaks ~3.5 h after the AM dose — matches the PK Tmax") is generated by comparing the observed lag-to-peak against the substance's known pharmacokinetic Tmax.
  • 9lag = PK Tmax matchA correlation lag that lines up with the substance's Tmax is the strongest signal the effect is causal rather than coincidental — that match is what earns the ACUTE good flag.
  • 1012-hour axisThe x-axis is a 12-hour clock (6A → 12A) so dose pins and effect ratings land where they were actually logged across the day.
