  • 1EIGHT DIMENSIONSOne compound, eight fixed facets — contents/route, bioavailability, mechanism, benefits, side effects, interactions, pharmacokinetics, long-term. Every substance card answers the same eight questions, so two compounds are always comparable head-to-head.
  • 2EVIDENCE TIERThe green strong-clinical badge in the header is the trust signal: it grades how well-supported the benefit claim is (strong-clinical > clinical > preliminary > anecdotal), so a prescribed stimulant doesn't read the same as an n=12 nootropic.
  • 3CLASS PILLThe Rx · Schedule II pill (vs OTC green / research-chem red) classifies legal + sourcing status at a glance. A scheduled prescription, an over-the-counter supplement, and a grey-market research chemical each carry a different pill color.
  • 4TABBED DEPTHFour tabs keep the card scannable: Overview is the eight-facet summary; Pharmacokinetics, Interactions, and Long-term each expand one dimension into its own deep view without burying the summary.
  • 5MECHANISM ROWThe mechanism facet carries the dopaminergic accent stripe (blue), tying this card back to the system color used in the chrono-timeline and neuro-load views — the same compound reads blue everywhere.
  • 6SIDE-EFFECT FLAGSide effects get the red flag stripe, the same accent as the regimen warning panel, so the two "watch-out" regions of the card read as a matched pair the eye lands on first.
  • 7PK MINI-CHARTThe decay curve under the grid is the metabolic window — peak ~10:30 AM, taper to ~11 PM — the visual the timeline's dose band is computed from. Tmax / t½ / duration sit in the mono caption beneath.
  • 8INTERACTION CHIPSThe four severity chips (major / moderate / good) summarise this compound's collisions in the user's actual stack. Each chip deep-links into the interaction heatmap, pre-filtered to the Lisdexamfetamine row.
  • 9MAOI CONTRAINDICATIONThe red major chip (9-Me-BC) and the regimen note both surface the live MAOI contraindication — the dossier doesn't just describe the drug, it flags the one dangerous pairing present right now in your stack.
  • 10REDUNDANCY COUNT"Appears in 1 regimen item" tells the user how many products in their stack contain this compound — the hook into the redundancy view, where a substance showing up in three products is the overlap to prune.
  • 11EDIT REGIMENThe ghost Edit-regimen affordance top-right jumps straight to the dose/timing editor for this compound, so reading the dossier and adjusting the actual schedule are one click apart.
