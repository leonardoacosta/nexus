EDIT AFFORDANCE Tap a bar to edit start/end, mark paused, or log why you stopped.
  • 1GANTT ROWEach row is one regimen_item with its status plotted over time — the long-term "what was I on, and when" record.
  • 2BAR / TRACKA filled bar spans the months the item was active; the 6-month window maps to 0–100% of the track width.
  • 3GAP = PAUSEDGaps between bars on one row are paused stretches — the on/off history (L-Tyrosine, Hydroxyzine PRN, Sleep blend).
  • 4COLOR = CLASSBar color encodes the item class: blue Rx, green OTC, red research-chem — same pill taxonomy as the dossier.
  • 5RX = CONTINUOUSPrescribed items (Lisdexamfetamine, Buspirone) run end-to-end; clinician-managed, not self-cycled.
  • 6RX = STARTED MIDA bar starting partway in (Guanfacine, Mar) marks an Rx added during the window, not the start of tracking.
  • 7CHEM TRIALResearch-chems show as short red bars (9-Me-BC, a brief Feb–Mar trial) — quick experiments, then stopped.
  • 8TODAY LINEThe dashed vertical near Jun marks today; bars touching it are still active, everything left of a bar's end is history.
  • 9ADD / EDIT"+ Add item" opens a new row; tapping any bar edits its start/end, marks it paused, or logs a stop reason.
  • 10RANGE PICKERThe "Jan – Jun 2026" control reframes the whole chart to a different window without losing item history.
  • 11COMPANION VIEWThis is the long-term counterpart to the daily chrono-timeline — months of regimen history, not a single day's doses.
